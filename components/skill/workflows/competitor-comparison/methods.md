# Select comparable evidence

For a single-product profile, use the supplied ASIN, whether it belongs to the seller or a competitor. Use dimensions 1 and 2 for product features and price, 4 for monthly purchases of the selected variant and its full listing. Take both lower bounds from the same monthly purchases response and group the listing observations by returned parent ASIN. Use dimension 3 for organic and sponsored visibility, and 5 for recurring buyer praise or complaints. Compare changes against that product’s own past.

For a teardown use the requested dates, defaulting to the last six complete months. Pass the study dates as `startDate` and `endDate` to [product history](../../datas/product-history/definition.md) for daily `aspects=["price"]`. Query monthly purchases by the corresponding months. Include a month-end value only if that month has ended by the report cutoff. For a mid-month cutoff include a provisional point only if its observation date is known to be on or before the cutoff. Otherwise stop the sales series at the preceding complete month. Label each observed period and compare only their overlap.

For an HTML teardown, pass the original daily price response to the renderer specified in the [task outline](report-outline.md). It calculates the observed changes from the dated prices. Leave the count in that chart note. For other formats, count nonzero differences between consecutive valid observations. A change spanning missing days counts once. Fewer than two valid prices leave the change count unknown. Coincident price and purchase changes alone do not establish a promotional effect.

1. For discovery, establish the target’s use and specifications from [product details](../../datas/product-detail/definition.md) or supplied materials. Use [product search](../../datas/product-discovery/definition.md) within the confirmed scope and record why each candidate is comparable.
2. For a standard comparison, use dimensions 1–4. For an explicitly deep comparison add dimension 5. For a focused request, identify the products and use only the requested measures. For differentiation, compare the relevant specifications and page facts with feedback about the same need.
3. For a requested external category benchmark, use [category profile](../../datas/market-profile/definition.md) or [category price bands](../../datas/market-price-distribution/definition.md) for the confirmed category. Label its population separately.
4. Resolve an ambiguous category with [category lookup](../../datas/category-lookup/definition.md) before comparing.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Product identity and comparability | ASIN, parent ASIN, product name, brand, category; available size, material, pack quantity and listing date needed to distinguish the offers | [product details](../../datas/product-detail/definition.md) | Establish what is being compared and the specification or pack differences affecting the comparison. |
| 2. Current commercial performance | Price and currency, monthly purchase/revenue lower bounds, star rating, rating count, seller count and variant count; ranking category with its BSR when relevant | [product details](../../datas/product-detail/definition.md) | Compare prices on the relevant specification and pack basis; calculate unit prices only with known compatible quantities. Keep child-ASIN measures and parent totals distinct. Compare BSR within the same ranking category. Describe observed price, sales and rating differences, rather than deriving profit from selling price. |
| 3. Exposure and traffic sources | Total estimated exposure, organic/sponsored exposure or shares and observed keyword coverage. For a comparison of contributing queries, also use each query's exposure, share and position. | [exposure sources](../../datas/traffic-structure/definition.md) · [contributing queries](../../datas/traffic-terms/definition.md) · [search placements](../../datas/search-placements/definition.md) | Compare source dependence and query coverage over the same period. Keep estimated visibility distinct from actual clicks, purchases or advertising spend. Term evidence describes ASIN-query relationships, not the contribution of title fragments. |
| 4. Performance changes | Monthly purchase/revenue lower bounds and returned adjacent-month growth; requested price, category-rank, rating-count or page-change observations; exposure trend when requested | [monthly purchases](../../datas/product-sales-trend/definition.md) · [product history](../../datas/product-history/definition.md) · [exposure trend](../../datas/traffic-trend/definition.md) · [weekly exposure](../../datas/traffic-weeks/definition.md) · [weekly ASIN-query visibility](../../datas/traffic-term-weeks/definition.md) | Keep current-month points separate. Other change measures are selected by the question. Use an abnormality label only with the user's explicit threshold or an applicable returned classification; otherwise describe the change. For ASIN-query changes, align exposure, position and query search volume or ABA rank; explain the supported movements without attributing them to an unmeasured cause. |
| 5. Buyer feedback and product differentiation | Each product's review population and period, same-theme mention counts/rates, associated ratings and the product/use conditions described; supporting excerpts when needed | [product review profile](../../datas/product-review-profile/definition.md) · [stored buyer reviews](../../datas/review-search/definition.md) · [latest page reviews](../../datas/review-live/definition.md) · [product details](../../datas/product-detail/definition.md) | Compare themes across distinct review populations over the same period. Identify the target product or page feature relevant to each documented concern. Keep shared-parent feedback at its supported variant scope. |

5. For traffic, use source shares for channel composition, contributor rows for the terms supplying exposure, and placement records only for a named query. For changes, start with the trend profile. Read weekly records only for an unresolved timing question. For a named query, use [weekly search demand](../../datas/query-weeks/definition.md) only for weeks where both search volume and ABA rank are missing from the visibility data and existing evidence.
6. Use the product review profile for competitor complaints, praise and requested improvements. Reuse one profile per confirmed parent and period. Present shared feedback once for siblings. If the parent relationship is unknown, keep findings scoped to their returned population and withhold variant-level differences. Select stored reviews for explicit quotation requests, or current page reviews for an explicit latest-page request.
