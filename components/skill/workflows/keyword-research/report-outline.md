# Arrange the keyword report

Identify the marketplace, seed or named queries, and actual measurement week or period once. Set the report's `task` to the branch used below. For a combined expansion and prioritization request, use `expansion` and close with the requested choices.

| Task | Reading order |
|---|---|
| `expansion` | State the candidate coverage and grouping basis. Use a narrow table per intent with one keyword per row and columns for relevance, estimated weekly searches and ABA search-frequency rank. When recommendations were requested, finish with each selected term and its deciding evidence. |
| `profile` | Start with the terms and their demand. Compare the requested dimensions side by side for the same week. Give each measure its own label and unit. Place the business meaning beside the difference it explains. For a baseline-only question, show just the requested statistics. |
| `trend` | Show the demand direction over the requested period, with start and end values and their change when available. Label ABA rank separately. For weekly series, keep missing weeks visible and use separate axes or charts for measures of different scales. |
| `asin-terms` | Identify the ASIN and show its observed terms, with the product's exposure and positions alongside the query's own demand. Keep organic and sponsored positions separate. For changes over time, show exposure, placement and query demand or ABA rank across aligned weeks. |

When the lookup used a translated seed, show the original and the search term used together in the scope. Keep returned keywords in their original language. Write headings, grade labels and findings in the conversation language.
