# Research a category market

1. Resolve category names with [category lookup](../../datas/category-lookup/definition.md). If a name identifies several markets, show full paths and wait for the seller’s selection. For an explicit brief to find opportunities within a parent, compare its peers at the requested level.
2. For a shortlist use [market discovery](../../datas/market-discovery/definition.md) with the requested scope and conditions. For a known market start from its [profile](../../datas/market-profile/definition.md). Compare whole-market demand and leading-sample metrics separately at consistent dates, levels and descendant scope.
3. Answer focused questions directly with the relevant [price bands](../../datas/market-price-distribution/definition.md), [product](../../datas/market-top-product-concentrations/definition.md), [brand](../../datas/market-brand-concentrations/definition.md), or [seller concentration](../../datas/market-seller-concentrations/definition.md). For other distributions choose the requested measure: [Star-rating distribution](../../datas/market-rating-distributions/definition.md), [Rating-count distribution](../../datas/market-rating-count-distributions/definition.md), [Listing-age distribution](../../datas/market-listing-age-distributions/definition.md), [Fulfillment distribution](../../datas/market-fulfillment-distributions/definition.md), [Seller-country distribution](../../datas/market-seller-country-distributions/definition.md), [Content distribution](../../datas/market-content-distributions/definition.md). For a trend use [market history](../../datas/market-history/definition.md). To rank categories by the number of recently launched products among their sales leaders, use [market discovery](../../datas/market-discovery/definition.md) in one batch of confirmed category IDs for the requested launch window and sales-ranked parent sample. Keep each category’s results separate. Pre-filtering a product search by listing age changes the population to bestsellers among new products and cannot supply this count. For listing ages, include parents whose first launch was 0 through the requested number of days before the ranking date. If the aggregate window differs, count qualifying parents in dated records from the same ranked sample. Rank categories by count only when the full sample is accounted for, retaining ties. With partial rows or unknown launch dates, report confirmed matches and their coverage. Use [launch composition](../../datas/market-launch-composition/definition.md) when the question asks when the leading listings first launched. A success rate requires the full launch cohort.
4. For unmet needs, use the [category review profile](../../datas/market-review-profile/definition.md) and corroborate specific concerns with available written reviews for identified examples. For an entry question, report demand and competition alongside the seller’s stated conditions. For examples within a target price range among newly ranked listings, read the [ranked listings](../../datas/market-top-product-concentrations/definition.md) for the same date, category scope and unit-sales sample. Filter the returned parents by first launch date and representative price. Report the matched parents and their categories. An empty partial sample leaves category-wide fit unresolved.

## Show how the newly ranked products developed

1. State the categories, launch window, ranking date and top-100 sales population. Compare peer categories when their new-listing counts cover the same full ranked sample scope. Include descendants within each category by default. When extending a report, retain its scope.
2. For included categories with confirmed matches or unknown counts, batch the [ranked listings](../../datas/market-top-product-concentrations/definition.md) at the same date and scope. Filter up to 20 returned listings per category by the parent's first launch date. Deduplicate parent ASINs across categories and select three by descending parent monthly unit sales. Exclude missing sales; label ties and use parent ASIN for a deterministic cutoff. Label the result “three highest-selling identified new listings”; claim an overall top three only with complete coverage. If fewer qualify, show all confirmed products and explain the coverage gap.
3. Resolve a representative product ASIN through [members of the ranked parent](../../datas/listing-members/definition.md). Use the [candidate assessment methods](methods.md) for monthly sales, daily price, weekly exposure and both daily category ranks. Use the [product trend panels](../../../tools/report/product-trends.md) and distinguish listing sales from the representative variant’s performance. End at the ranking date and look back up to six months, starting no earlier than launch. Compare completed months and label any available partial month through its observation date.

## Compare growing markets

1. Confirm the category scope. Use [category lookup](../../datas/category-lookup/definition.md) to list a named parent’s direct children, or all root categories for an explicit all-category comparison. Compare peers at one level, including descendants consistently. For a named leaf, assess its own history; ask before adding peers. If coverage exceeds budget, agree on a narrower scope before reading history.
2. Use [market history](../../datas/market-history/definition.md). For a named month or “last month”, fix that month and its preceding month before comparing categories. For an undated request, use the latest consecutive complete months with comparable data and examine six complete months for context. Apply the same pair to every category. If either month is missing or the baseline is nonpositive, identify the excluded category and keep the comparison months fixed. Show both monthly sales totals from `marketSize.monthlySales`, their difference and percentage change. Rank positive changes by percentage and retain ties.
3. Use earlier months to distinguish a one-month jump, a rebound and repeated gains. A small base can produce a large percentage. Make a market finding only from category observations. If no category has comparable positive growth, deliver that result and stop.
4. When the seller requests representative products for leading growth markets, use [product concentration](../../datas/market-top-product-concentrations/definition.md) at the same month end, inclusion scope and `unitSalesTop100` sample. Keep the returned parent ASIN, family unit sales, representative price, stars and rating count on each parent row. For the top three markets, default to one leading listing per market unless a different count is requested. Reuse verified parent titles, brands and images. For missing visuals or identity text, reuse the first member from the [listing members](../../datas/listing-members/definition.md) read for the same month (page 1, size 1), ordered by monthly purchase lower bound descending. Fill available photo, title and brand from that member and pass their field names in `representativeFields`. Keep the row at parent scope. Use missing values if enrichment is unavailable. Sample shares describe the leading sample. If that date or list is unavailable, keep the market finding and mark the examples unavailable. Individual examples illustrate what sells in the market. Their presence or a simultaneous price drop cannot establish what caused category growth.
